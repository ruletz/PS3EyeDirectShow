PS3 Eye Windows Virtual Camera
==============================

This camera comes from PS3EyeVCamSource.dll (the Media Foundation source) and
PS3EyeCaptureService (the service that owns the PS3 Eye over USB).

Quick check after installing:

  1. Open the Windows Camera app, or
     https://webrtc.github.io/samples/src/content/devices/input-output/
     and pick "PS3 Eye Windows Virtual Camera".
  2. If the camera is missing:
       "%ProgramFiles%\PS3 Eye\PS3EyeVCamRegistrar.exe" status
  3. If the camera is listed but the picture is black:
       sc query PS3EyeCaptureService
     the service must be running, and the PS3 Eye must not be held by OBS or by
     the DirectShow filter (it is a single-client USB device).
  4. Install log: %ProgramData%\PS3 Eye\install.log

Uninstall via Settings > Apps, or:

  powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "%ProgramFiles%\PS3 Eye\Install-PS3EyeNative.ps1" -Uninstall
